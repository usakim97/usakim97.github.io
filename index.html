Self-Hosted Service Stack & Training-Data Pipeline

Ongoing

I run a multi-service Docker Compose deployment — FastAPI, ChromaDB, SearXNG, ntfy, Cloudflare tunnel — reachable over Tailscale, and contribute media ingest, transcription, and UI fixes to the open-source project it is built on. On top of it I designed a training-data pipeline for video and audio: clips are cut cheaply first, held for human review, and captioned on the GPU only after approval, so no model time is spent on footage that gets rejected. Reviewers type plain notes like mute 2.5-3.0s; crop and the pipeline parses and applies them with ffmpeg before captioning.

Docker ComposeFastAPIPython ffmpegGPU SchedulingWhisper pyannoteTailscale

Why the gate exists

Captioning is the expensive step: a vision model plus speech-to-text on the GPU. Running it before a human looks at the footage means paying for clips that get thrown away. Moving the review in front of the GPU stage was the design decision — watch the rejected clip stop at the gate.
